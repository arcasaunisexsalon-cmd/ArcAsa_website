About Us

Built by stylists who were tired of rushed appointments.

Copper & Comb started as a single chair in a shared studio, run by three colorists who believed a good cut starts with a real conversation. Thirteen years later, we run six neighborhood studios — each one small enough to know your name, and skilled enough to handle the technical work of a downtown flagship.

We train continuously, source products we'd use ourselves, and keep every studio unhurried by design: fewer chairs, longer slots, better outcomes.
